# Original CLEA7 material library

The Studio Materials panel supplies 12 editable compositions, 8 original colour
palettes and 6 bounded animation recipes. Previews use the actual application
renderer. They contain no uploaded photos, stock images, external fonts, copied
UI templates, downloaded artwork or mandatory AI call.

Interactive compositions include real navigation, local notes, table records,
condition checks, disclosures, a bounded stepper and a state-machine lifecycle.
Typography and geometry entries are explicitly design material. Composition
insertion materializes independent components, state, flows and required screens;
users edit the ordinary public App DNA model. It is undoable. Palettes change
presentation without replacing logic. Motion recipes create actual timeline
tracks and an animation flow to wire to an event; they do not invent behavior.
Reduced-motion support and existing animation budgets remain in force.

`MATERIALS-LICENSE.txt` grants MIT reuse of the original material design data,
including commercial apps. Keep the notice with substantial copies. The Studio,
Runtime and SDK implementation retain their separate distribution terms.
The library does not establish rights to third-party material a user later adds.
AI-generated assets, if later introduced, need recorded provider terms and review
for third-party rights; generation alone does not establish legal clearance.

App DNA /4 carries optional `licenses`, an array of at most 20 unique
`{id, name, text}` entries. IDs follow ordinary application identifier rules;
names are at most 100 characters and notice text at most 16,000 characters.
Entries are data, never executable HTML or fetched URLs. SDK/browser validators
reject duplicate IDs, path-like IDs, malformed entries and old ABI understatement.
Licences are covered by the package payload integrity/signature. They are also
retained in HTML consumer notices and generated Windows/Android source archives.
The original material notice is inserted once when materials, palettes or motion
recipes are used. Removing a notice does not revoke the corresponding obligations.

Evidence comes from `tests/test_materials.cjs`, independent Python contract/export
validation in `scripts/check_material_contract.py`, real Chrome material editor
checks, and the signed `sdk_materials` Android instrumentation fixture. A fixture
publisher is not a customer or a verified organisation. Physical low-end device
performance and paid AI calls are separate checks.
